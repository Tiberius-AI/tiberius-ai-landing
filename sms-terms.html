SMS Terms

Tiberius Account Notifications · Last updated: September 9, 2026

About this program

Tiberius AI LLC sends recurring first-party account and operational notifications to existing Tiberius users who separately choose to receive them.

Messages include account status and security notices, service availability and maintenance notices, billing or subscription events, and workflow review or approval reminders.

This program does not include marketing, promotions, lending, age-gated content, or contractor-to-homeowner messaging.

Your choice to receive messages

Enrollment uses the Tiberius SMS consent page, where you provide your mobile number and select a separate, initially unchecked consent checkbox. Consent is not a condition of purchase.

Enrollment is currently closed. When enrollment is available, a submitted number will remain pending until the recurring-program enrollment confirmation has been sent and recorded.

Frequency and charges

Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Contact your wireless provider for information about your plan.

Stop messages or get help

Reply STOP to unsubscribe. Reply HELP for help, or contact support@tiberius.ai.

Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Your privacy

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent are not shared with third parties, except aggregators and providers of the text message services.

Read the Privacy Policy for more information about how Tiberius handles your information.
