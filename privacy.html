All the above categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties, excluding aggregators and providers of the text message services.

Connected Business Accounts and Alfred

If you connect QuickBooks, Tiberius may receive selected company information, customer names and contact details, products or services and pricing, and invoice information needed to answer your requests and prepare private drafts.

Alfred uses Anthropic to generate AI responses. Your questions, relevant conversation and business context, and selected results from connected accounts may be sent to Anthropic for that purpose. QuickBooks authorization credentials are handled separately by the server and are not included in Alfred's model prompts.

If you use microphone input, audio is sent to ElevenLabs for transcription. If you enable spoken replies, the response text is sent to ElevenLabs to generate speech. That text may include information from your connected business accounts.

Supabase provides storage and backend services, and Vercel hosts the website. Depending on the services you use, providers such as Twilio, Retell and payment processors may also process information to provide messaging, telephone or payment services.

Disconnecting QuickBooks stops new access. A completed disconnection removes the stored authorization credentials, but it does not automatically delete saved conversations or private drafts. To request deletion of your information, contact support@tiberius.ai.
