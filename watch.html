Straight Answers on Ownership and Monthly Care

What does “owning my website outright” actually mean?

When you buy your site outright on Day 1 (or finish your 12 monthly Rent-to-Own payments), the website, design, custom calculators, and setup belong to you. You never owe another lease payment. If you ever want to move your website to your own hosting account, we hand everything over to you with no buyout fees or penalties.

What is the optional $100/month Web & AI Care Plan?

Owning your website is like owning your work truck, while the Care Plan covers the fuel, phone lines, and maintenance that keep it running on the road. Every time your website texts a missed caller, analyzes a customer's job photo, or sends a review request, it uses live telecom phone lines, text messaging carriers, and AI models that charge per use. For $100 a month, we cover your high-speed hosting, dedicated texting number, carrier registration, AI usage fees, security updates, and routine text or photo edits on your site.

Do I have to use the $100/month Web & AI Care Plan once I own my site?

No. It is completely optional. Most business owners choose the $100/month plan because they do not want to deal with hosting servers, telecom compliance, or AI billing accounts. However, once your website is paid off, you are free to host the site yourself and plug in your own phone and AI provider accounts at cost.

What happens if I need to cancel during the 12-month Rent-to-Own plan?

There are no long-term lock-in penalties. Plus, your first 30 days are protected by our 30-Day 100% Money-Back Guarantee. You can pay off your remaining balance at any time to own your website immediately, or cancel with 30 days' notice if your business plans change.
